## Idea
When the first frost is forecast, every tomato still green is picked. Ripe ones are eaten fresh; green ones are cooked into chutney within three days.

## Why it matters
Basil plans one chutney session per autumn and asks Fern for the expected green-tomato weight a week ahead.
