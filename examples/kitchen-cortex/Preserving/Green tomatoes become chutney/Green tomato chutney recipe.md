## Content
1. Chop 1 kg green tomatoes and 2 onions.
2. Add 250 g brown sugar, 250 ml cider vinegar, 1 tsp salt, mustard seed.
3. Simmer uncovered 90 minutes, stirring, until thick.
4. Jar hot in sterilised jars; keeps 12 months.
